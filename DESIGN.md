# Visual direction

Preserve the 3D room, typography, scroll mechanics, lighting, adaptive quality, and responsive framing. Café & Cadence remains the decorative café name.

Sage walls (#536657 / #3e5046), warm timber, espresso backgrounds (#140d09), milk text (#f7f1e8), amber highlights (#efc17a), and pale sage interaction accents (#b6d8a9). Instrument Serif for display, Geist for body, Geist Mono for controls.

Nine camera chapters: welcome, first coding at the chessboard, school at the Python notebook, NOC at the Vietnam ticket, project folders on the laptop, experience at the receipt printer, AABW at the trophy/photo wall, a scripted question guide, and evening coffee. Preserve the bicycle and its interactive wheels. Each chapter is a separate room object, not a nested flipbook.

Keep the scenic story conversational and human. Technical detail belongs in the laptop project details, experience, and recruiter view. NOC/hackathon photos will arrive later; existing frames are labeled placeholders. The question guide uses authored replies without a model or credentials.

The entry choices use unequal emphasis: a compact warm amber scenic-route button in Instrument Serif, paired with a quiet underlined Detour link. Parenthetical notes sit below in italic serif. Keep this pairing simple and typographic, without equal-sized cards, glows, or monospace button labels.
