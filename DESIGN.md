# Visual direction

An adaptation of the supplied 3D portfolio, preserving the incumbent interface and composition. Café & Cadence is a working theme, not a final personal brand.

The room uses sage green walls (#536657 / #3e5046), warm timber, espresso backgrounds (#140d09), milk text (#f7f1e8), amber highlights (#efc17a), and pale sage interaction accents (#b6d8a9). Instrument Serif remains the display face, with Geist for body text and Geist Mono for interface labels.

The existing eight-chapter scroll journey remains. The second chapter now frames an interactive road bicycle, with desktop and portrait camera keys. The route card, laptop, receipt printer, photo wall, café notebook, and final evening scene carry the rest of the story. This is real WebGL geometry; retain the source's adaptive quality and reduced-motion alternative.

Keep empty personal links hidden. Project and biographical spaces must remain explicitly labeled until real content is supplied. The notebook is a scripted guide during this stage.
