# Visual direction

Preserve the 3D room, scroll mechanics, lighting, adaptive quality, and responsive framing. Café & Cadence remains the decorative café name.

Sage walls (#536657 / #3e5046), warm timber, espresso backgrounds (#140d09), milk text (#f7f1e8), amber highlights (#efc17a), and pale sage interaction accents (#b6d8a9). Lora at weight 500 for headings, Newsreader at weight 400 for story paragraphs, Geist for technical detail and controls. Keep Instrument Serif on physical café objects and Geist Mono on the ticket, receipt, and terminal. Story headings use 34px on mobile and 40px on desktop, with 18/19px paragraphs at 1.6 line height. Use a narrow 24rem reading column, normal paragraph spacing, and quiet underlined links. Avoid oversized headings, uppercase story kickers, highlighted secondary paragraphs, and pill-shaped story links.

Nine camera chapters: welcome, first coding at the chessboard, school at the Python notebook, NOC at the Vietnam ticket, project folders on the laptop, experience at the receipt printer, AABW at the trophy/photo wall, a scripted question guide, and evening coffee. Preserve the bicycle and its interactive wheels. Each chapter is a separate room object, not a nested flipbook.

Keep the scenic story conversational and human. Technical detail belongs in the laptop project details, experience, and recruiter view. NOC/hackathon photos will arrive later; existing frames are labeled placeholders. The question guide uses authored replies without a model or credentials.

The entry choices use unequal emphasis: a compact warm amber scenic-route button in Lora, paired with a quiet underlined Detour link. Parenthetical notes sit below in italic serif. Keep this pairing simple and typographic, without equal-sized cards, glows, or monospace button labels.
